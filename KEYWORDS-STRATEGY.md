## Keyword Usage Guidelines

### Title Tags
- Include primary keyword at the beginning
- Keep under 60 characters
- Make it compelling and clickable

Example: "Kunphen Hospital - Tibetan Medicine & Sowa Rigpa Center in [City]"

### Meta Descriptions
- Include primary and 1-2 secondary keywords
- Keep under 160 characters
- Include a call-to-action
- Mention location

Example: "Experience authentic Tibetan medicine at Kunphen Hospital in [City]. Expert Sowa Rigpa practitioners, herbal remedies, pulse diagnosis. Book your consultation today!"

### Headers (H1, H2, H3)
- H1: Include primary keyword (only one H1 per page)
- H2: Include secondary keywords naturally
- H3: Use long-tail variations

### Content Body
- Use keywords naturally (keyword density: 1-2%)
- Include synonyms and related terms
- Use keywords in first 100 words
- Bold important keywords occasionally

### Image Alt Text
- Describe the image accurately
- Include relevant keywords when natural
- Keep under 125 characters

Example: "Tibetan medicine practitioner performing pulse diagnosis at Kunphen Hospital"

## Avoiding Keyword Stuffing

❌ **Don't Do This:**
"Kunphen Hospital is the best Kunphen Hospital for Tibetan medicine. Our Kunphen Hospital offers Tibetan medicine services."

✅ **Do This:**
"Kunphen Hospital specializes in authentic Tibetan medicine and Sowa Rigpa healing. Our experienced practitioners offer traditional treatments including pulse diagnosis and herbal remedies."
